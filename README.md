# Cybersecurity Portfolio

Welcome to my cybersecurity portfolio.

This repository documents my ongoing development in cybersecurity through technical learning, hands-on practice, practical projects, and independent study. It is intended to serve as a record of my progress and a demonstration of the knowledge and technical skills I develop throughout my cybersecurity journey.

## Portfolio Contents

### Notes

Technical notes and summaries created from my studies, hands-on practice, and cybersecurity learning platforms.

Topics may include cybersecurity fundamentals, networking, operating systems, security concepts, tools, techniques, and other areas of technical learning.

### Projects

Practical cybersecurity and programming projects designed to strengthen my technical skills and demonstrate applied knowledge.

Projects may include security-focused exercises, scripts, applications, and other technical work developed as part of my learning.

### Home Lab

Documentation of my personal cybersecurity lab environment.

This section will document lab setup procedures, configurations, experiments, troubleshooting, observations, and lessons learned from practical security exercises.

### Incident Analysis

Analysis of cybersecurity incidents, attack scenarios, security events, and investigative exercises.

These analyses will focus on understanding how incidents occur, identifying relevant security concepts and techniques, examining potential indicators and impacts, and documenting lessons learned.

### Certifications

Study plans, notes, and progress related to cybersecurity certifications.

This section will be used to document certification preparation and supporting learning material as I work toward relevant industry certifications.

## Purpose

The purpose of this portfolio is to:

- Document my cybersecurity learning and development.
- Apply theoretical knowledge through practical work.
- Strengthen my technical and analytical skills.
- Build experience with cybersecurity tools, technologies, and techniques.
- Track my progress over time.
- Provide prospective employers with insight into my technical development and areas of interest.

## Current Focus

I am currently developing my foundational cybersecurity knowledge through structured learning and hands-on practice. My goal is to progressively expand this portfolio as I develop more practical experience and work toward an entry-level career in cybersecurity and security operations.

## Disclaimer

All security testing and experimentation documented in this repository is conducted in authorized environments, such as personal lab environments, intentionally vulnerable systems, or platforms that explicitly permit security testing.

The material in this repository is intended for educational and defensive purposes.
